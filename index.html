The Problem

You’re only as good as who you refer.

The barrier to entry in real estate is low. Accountability is lower. Referrals happen on gut feel, hearsay, and hope — and one bad referral can cost you a client, a reputation, or a deal.

There has never been a real, industry-internal way to know who’s actually competent, ethical, and worth trusting.

Until now.

Who you refer says everything about you. The problem is nobody has real data on who’s actually good — just Facebook groups, guesswork, and the same names getting passed around.

— The reason VouchTerra exists
How It Works

What Makes Us Different

Four principles we won’t break.

A trust platform lives or dies by what it refuses to compromise. Here’s what we’ll never do.

01

The badge is never for sale.

You can’t buy Verified. You can’t buy Trusted. You can’t buy Elite. Every credential is earned through verified transactions and honest ratings — or it isn’t real.

02

Only real transactions rate.

No anonymous complaints. No sockpuppet reviews. If you haven’t actually done a deal with someone, you can’t rate them. That’s what makes our ratings worth something.

03

Ads never touch ratings.

Promoted placement is clearly labeled and always separate from organic rankings. Paying us more never changes what people say about your work.

04

The base network is free forever.

Every professional gets a free profile, referral tracking, and access to the network. Trust needs density to work — and density needs free.

Built with RESPA in mind.

VouchTerra tracks referrals and outcomes — never referral fees. There is no place in the product to record, request, or pay anything of value for a referral to a lender, title or escrow company, inspector, or any other settlement service provider, and there never will be. Reputations here are earned from completed work and honest ratings, not from what changes hands. This is a design principle, not legal advice — talk to your broker or counsel about your own obligations.
