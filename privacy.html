Privacy Policy

Effective September 5, 2026

VouchTerra ("VouchTerra," "we," "us") is a verified professional network for the real estate industry, operated from Tacoma, Washington. This policy explains what information we collect when you use vouchterra.com, how we use it, and the choices you have. We wrote it to be read, not skimmed past. If anything is unclear, email go@vouchterra.com.

What we collect

Information you give us. When you join the waitlist or create an account, we collect your name, email address, professional role (for example, agent, lender, inspector), and the market you work in. Licensed real estate agents and brokers may optionally provide a Washington license number so we can verify it. If you contact us, we keep the message and your contact details.

Information from sign-in providers. If you sign in with Google or Microsoft, the provider shares your name, email address, and profile picture with us. We do not receive your password, and we do not get access to your email, contacts, calendar, or files.

Information collected automatically. Like most websites, our hosting provider records basic technical information such as IP address, browser type, and pages visited, used for security and to keep the site running. We do not currently run advertising trackers.

How we use it

  • To create and maintain your account and Founding Member status.
  • To verify professional credentials against public licensing records.
  • To contact you about VouchTerra: your account, product updates, and when the platform opens in your market. You can opt out of non-essential emails at any time.
  • To operate, secure, and improve the service.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with advertisers. Your Verified status on VouchTerra is earned through real transactions and is never for sale.

What is public

VouchTerra is a professional network, so once profiles launch, your name, role, market, and verification status are intended to be visible to other verified members. Your email address, phone number, and license number are never shown publicly. Reviews tied to transactions display only the city or neighborhood and month/year of the transaction, never a property's street address.

Who we share it with

We use a small number of service providers to run VouchTerra, and they process your data only on our instructions: Supabase (account database and authentication, hosted in the United States), Vercel (website hosting), Formspree (waitlist form processing), and Google and Microsoft (sign-in, if you choose them). We may also disclose information if required by law, or to protect the rights and safety of VouchTerra and its members.

How long we keep it

We keep your account information for as long as your account exists. If you delete your account, we remove your profile and personal information within 30 days, except for records we are required to keep by law and anonymized data that no longer identifies you. Your Founding Member number is retired and never reissued.

Your choices

  • Access and correction. You can view and edit your profile at any time after signing in.
  • Deletion. Email go@vouchterra.com from your account address and we will delete your account.
  • Email. Every non-essential email includes an unsubscribe link.
  • Sign-in providers. You can revoke VouchTerra's access from your Google or Microsoft account settings at any time.

Security

Your data is stored with providers that use encryption in transit and at rest, and our database is configured so that each member can only access their own records. No system is perfectly secure, so if you believe your account has been compromised, contact us right away.

Children

VouchTerra is for working professionals and is not directed at anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from minors.

Changes

If we make meaningful changes to this policy, we will update the effective date above and, for significant changes, notify account holders by email.
